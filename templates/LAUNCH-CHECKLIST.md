# Launch checklist and screenshot manifest

Complete in a private hotel project. Mark only after checking the saved state.

- [ ] Correct account, property and authority confirmed; duplicates reviewed.
- [ ] Budget scope, taxes/fees and same-day history resolved.
- [ ] Approved budget and bid controls saved and reopened.
- [ ] Correct markets, language, network options, schedule and time zone.
- [ ] Keywords and negatives match hotel booking intent.
- [ ] AI expansion settings and confirmation dialogs verified.
- [ ] Official property destination works on mobile.
- [ ] Genuine photo rights, crops and claims checked.
- [ ] Correct phone and only this hotel's location asset.
- [ ] No unrelated inherited sitelinks, calls or locations.
- [ ] Primary goals do not mislabel clicks or directions as bookings.
- [ ] Start date, campaign status and policy status reopened after publication.
- [ ] Preview distinguished from observed delivery; no own-ad clicks.
- [ ] Private handoff identifies next action, owner and review date.
