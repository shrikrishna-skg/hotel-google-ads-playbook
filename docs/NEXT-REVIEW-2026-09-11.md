## Bidding decision

Keep the current focused Search approach and approved spend boundary while gathering evidence. A single reported conversion does not justify switching bidding strategy, increasing budgets or declaring a winning hotel. If delivery remains weak, diagnose keyword eligibility, search demand, location settings and bid limitations before changing one variable. Do not split this small budget into additional campaigns merely to test copy.

All-day eligibility is a scheduling choice, not a promise of continuous impressions. Correct phone, address, policy and spending issues promptly; the review window is not a reason to leave a known error in place.

## Reporting correction and budget safeguard

The campaign table's Cost value is not proof of the final amount charged. An unusually high historical figure needs reconciliation before describing it as a billing overcharge. Today's budget also does not establish the budget that applied on an earlier day.

For most eligible campaigns, an unchanged $2.50 average daily budget corresponds to a $5 daily billed-ad-cost limit. Verify campaign exceptions, higher same-day budget changes and all campaigns assigned to the property. Taxes and fees are separate. Do not promise a $5 all-in bank charge or use a delayed pause rule as a guaranteed hard cap. [Google spending limits](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637?hl=en), [budget changes](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10487143?hl=en).
