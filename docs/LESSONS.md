# Lessons from the Gallatin hotel setup

Historical process lessons from September 9, 2026. This page intentionally omits client account identifiers, private reports, live status and internal document links.

## Budget language caused the largest misunderstanding

The owner's “maximum per day” needed translation into the platform's average-budget model. Agree the exact billing boundary first, document it beside the setting, and distinguish reported served cost from final billed cost. Do not use intraday budget reductions as retroactive protection.

## One property's ad can inherit another business's assets

A setup preview surfaced unrelated marketing sitelinks and other property locations. The reusable fix is to inspect association levels and use a property-only location group. Preserve valid associations before changing shared scope. Preview the final result again.

## A toggle is not proof of the saved setting

The wizard summary and intended expansion controls did not initially agree. Reopening the published settings exposed a confirmation step. Complete the dialog, save, then reopen again. Store final-state evidence after any correction.

## Real photographs explain the stay

The room and exterior gave guests more useful information than a generic promotional graphic. Keep crops faithful to the original property. Strong visual appeal is a reasonable starting hypothesis, not proof of higher conversion.

## Measurement must follow the real booking path

The chain's booking engine and the advertising account are separate systems. Website clicks and local actions cannot be relabeled as completed bookings. Coordinate with the booking provider and front desk to close that gap.

## Documentation should be reproducible

Use numbered captures and captions. Wait for each screen/image insertion to finish, then verify order and completeness. Keep the first page concise; put detailed evidence in an appendix. Check the actual exported font and embedded images when producing a Google Doc/Word handoff.

## Do not confuse visibility channels

An organic hotel listing, a chain advertisement and this property's campaign are different evidence. Search, Maps discovery and Hotel Center connectivity need separate verification. A single personal search is not a full delivery audit.
